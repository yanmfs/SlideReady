# Code of conduct

Be kind and assume good faith. Criticize the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and discrimination of any kind are not welcome in issues, pull requests or discussions of SlideReady.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads and block people who do not follow this. To raise a concern, use the private contact on the profile of the repository owner.

This is a short version in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
